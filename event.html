EVENT DETAILS

More than a tournament.

PACE is designed to give college coaches a complete look at each player — practice habits, ball striking, short game, competitive decision-making, composure, and on-course performance.

DAY 1 · SATURDAY

Evaluation Day

Structured practice-facility rotations with college coaches observing players across multiple parts of the game.

  • Full-swing evaluation
  • Short-game evaluation
  • Putting evaluation
  • Coach observation and interaction
DAY 2 · SUNDAY

Competition Day

18 holes of competitive golf with college coaches circulating throughout the course and evaluating players in a real tournament environment.

  • 18-hole competitive round
  • On-course coach evaluation
  • Official scoring
  • Closing player/coach access
WHO IT'S FOR Competitive male junior golfers

Players actively exploring Division II and Division III college golf opportunities.

COACH FOCUS DII & DIII Men's Golf

Regional, academically respected, and nationally competitive programs.
