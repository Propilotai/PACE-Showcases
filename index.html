WHY PACE

Recruiting is about more than rankings and scoring averages.

Scores matter. Rankings matter. But they do not tell a coach everything about a player. PACE gives college coaches the opportunity to see junior golfers compete, practice, communicate, respond to adversity, and carry themselves in person.

Our focus is simple: create meaningful access between prospective student-athletes and Division II and Division III golf programs.

THE SHOWCASE EXPERIENCE

Built for evaluation. Designed for connection.

01

Player Evaluation

Structured practice-facility evaluation gives coaches a closer look at the full player — not just a tournament score.

02

Competitive Golf

Players compete on-course while coaches circulate, observe decision-making, and evaluate performance in a real golf setting.

03

Coach Access

PACE is intentionally centered on Division II and Division III programs, creating a more focused and useful recruiting environment.
